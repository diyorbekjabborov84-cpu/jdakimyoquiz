# 6.2. HF — ftorid kislota — manba va rasm izohi

## Manbalar (to‘liq o‘qilgan)
1. **Housecroft & Sharpe, Inorganic Chemistry — 9.7A: Liquid Hydrogen Fluoride** (https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Inorganic_Chemistry/Map:_Inorganic_Chemistry_(Housecroft)/09:_Non-aqueous_Media/9.07:_Liquid_Hydrogen_Fluoride/9.7A:_Physical_Properties). Suyuqlanish diapazoni 190–292,5 K; nisbiy o‘tkazuvchanlik ε = 84 (273 K) va 175 (200 K); o‘z-o‘zini ionlashuv K_self = 2×10⁻¹² (273 K); 3HF ⇌ H₂F⁺ + HF₂⁻.
2. **Barron’s, Chemistry of the Main Group Elements — 10.2: Compounds of Fluorine** (https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Inorganic_Chemistry/Chemistry_of_the_Main_Group_Elements_(Barron)/10:_The_Halogens/10.02:_Compounds_of_Fluorine). HF da vodorod bog‘i va polimerlanish; ε = 84,2; HF₂⁻ hosil bo‘lishi (pK = 0,7); SbF₅ bilan superkislota; BF₃, PF₅, AlF₃, GeF₄, SnF₄, SeF₄ — fluorid qabul qiluvchilar.
3. **Wikipedia — Hydrogen fluoride / Hydrofluoric acid.** Kuchsiz kislota sababi — vodorod bog‘langan ion juftlari [H₃O⁺·F⁻]; konsentratsiyalangan eritmada bifluorid ionlari; 3HF ⇌ H₂F⁺ + HF₂⁻; SiO₂ + 6HF → H₂SiF₆ + 2H₂O; SiO₂ + 4HF → SiF₄ + 2H₂O; azeotrop 37 % / 120 °C; M = 20,006 g/mol.
4. **Wikipedia — Superacid; Fluoroantimonic acid; Antimony pentafluoride.** H₀(HF) = −15,1; H₀(SbF₅) = −21; 1:1 aralashma H₀ < −28; SbF₅ + 2HF ⇌ H₂F⁺ + SbF₆⁻; real eritma spetsiatsiyasi (H₃F₂⁺, Sb₄F₂₁⁻).
5. **Kim et al. (2024), Applied Surface Science — Understanding the contributions of F⁻, HF and HF₂⁻ to the etching of SiO₂.** Tezlik qonuni: r (Å/min) = 510[F⁻] + 610[HF] + 2890[HF₂⁻]; HF₂⁻ — asosiy hissa.
6. **Helms et al.; Yu et al.; GlobSino (etch ma’lumotlari).** HF₂⁻ tezligi HF dan ~4 barobar yuqori; H₂SiF₆ to‘planishi yemirishni sekinlashtiradi; selekтивlik SiO₂ : Si₃N₄ : Si ≈ 1 : >100 : 0,1; LPCVD Si₃N₄ — 3,3 Å/s; alyuminiy filmida fluoratsiya qatlami ~2000 Å, >50 % F.
7. **CDC/NIOSH — Hydrogen Fluoride (MMG 1142); Calpoison — Hydrofluoric Acid and Fluorides; StatPearls (NCBI) — Hydrofluoric Acid Toxicity; American Chemistry Association — Anhydrous HF ko‘rsatmalari.** F⁺ Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺ ni bog‘lashi, gipokalsemiya/gipomagneziya/giperkaliemiya, kechikuvchi og‘riq, kalsiy glyukonat; OSHA PEL TWA 3 ppm, ACGIH TLV-TWA 0,5 ppm (F), TLV-Ceiling 2 ppm, NIOSH IDLH 30 ppm.
8. **WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality, 4th ed. — Fluoride fact sheet (2022); US EPA — Fluoride in Drinking Water.** Qo‘llanma qiymati 1,5 mg/L; AQSh MCL 4,0 mg/L, ikkilamchi standart 2,0 mg/L. Chiqindi boshqaruv: 2HF + Ca(OH)₂ → CaF₂↓ + 2H₂O; aluminiy gidroksidi bilan AlF₃ cho‘kmasi.

## Model va hisoblar (barchasi ochiq shartli, o‘lchov ma’lumoti emas)
- **Model A — suvli HF spetsiatsiyasi** (ideal erituvchi modeli; Ka = 6,8×10⁻⁴, HF + F⁻ ⇌ HF₂⁻ lgK = 0,7; zaryad va massa balansi bo‘yicha sonikli yechim):
  0,010 M → HF 76,0 %, F⁻ 22,3 %, HF₂⁻ 0,8 %, pH 2,635; 0,050 M → 86,0 / 9,8 / 2,1 %, pH 2,225; 0,100 M → 87,9 / 6,4 / 2,8 %, pH 2,033; 0,500 M → 89,4 / 1,9 / 4,3 %, pH 1,503; 1,000 M → 89,5 / 1,1 / 4,7 %, pH 1,238; 2,0 M → pH 0,958; 5,0 M → pH 0,573; 10,000 M → HF₂⁻ 5,2 %, pH 0,277.
  Model chegarasi: faolik koeffitsiyentlar, pastki suv faolligi va ion juftlarining vodorod bog‘lanishi hisobga olinmaydi — shuning uchun adabiyotdagi «konsentratsiyalangan HF da bifluorid ustun» xulosasi modelda to‘liq namoyon bo‘lmaydi.
- **Model B — shishani yemirish**: SiO₂ + 6HF → H₂SiF₆ + 2H₂O; 1 kg SiO₂ uchun HF 1997,8 g (48 % li eritmada 4162,1 g), H₂SiF₆ 2398,2 g, H₂O 599,6 g. Tezlik modeli: 0,05 M → 32 Å/min; 0,10 M → 65,1; 0,25 M → 167; 0,50 M → 340,3; 1,000 M → 687,8; 2,000 M → 1384; 4,000 M → 2778,7; 8,000 M → 5567,7 Å/min; HF₂⁻ ulushi 13 → 21 %.
- **Model C — passivatsiya** (1 g metall): Al → AlF₃ 3,113 g (HF 1,483 g, H₂ 0,112 g); Fe → FeF₃ 2,326 g (HF 0,716 g, H₂ 0,054 g); Ni → NiF₂ 1,647 g (HF 0,682 g, H₂ 0,017 g); Cu → CuF₂ 1,598 g (HF 0,630 g, H₂ 0,016 g).
- **Model D — chiqindi boshqaruv**: 2HF + Ca(OH)₂ → CaF₂↓ + 2H₂O; 1000 kg 10 % li HF uchun Ca(OH)₂ 185,2 kg, CaF₂ 195,1 kg, H₂O 90,0 kg; kirish 285,2 kg = chiqish 285,2 kg. Qoldiq fluorid (K_sp(CaF₂) = 3,9×10⁻¹¹): Ca²⁺ 0,001 M → 3,752 mg/L; 0,005 M → 1,68 mg/L; 0,010 M → 1,186 mg/L; 0,050 M → 0,53 mg/L; 0,100 M → 0,375 mg/L. Chegara: WHO 1,5 mg/L.
- **Model E — kaltsiy bog‘lash stoxiometriyasi**: 20 g HF → 1,00 mol HF → 0,500 mol Ca²⁺ → 20,03 g Ca → 224,1 g kalsiy glyukonat (55,5 g CaCl₂) va 39,0 g CaF₂; 100 g HF → 2,499 mol Ca²⁺ → 100,16 g Ca → 1120,6 g glyukonat.
- **Model F — olish**: 1000 kg 97 % fluorit, 92 % unum → HF 457,3 kg, H₂SO₄ 1218,5 kg, CaSO₄ 1691,4 kg.
- **Anhidr HF konstantalari**: Tb 19,5 °C; Tm −83,6 °C; suyuqlanish 190–292,5 K; ε = 84 (273 K), 175 (200 K); K_self = 2×10⁻¹²; Λ_eq = 1,4×10⁻⁵ Ω⁻¹; μ = 1,9 D; ΔH_vap = 30,7 kJ/mol; ρ = 0,99 g/cm³; H₀ = −15,1 (SbF₅ bilan −21).

## Cheklovlar
- Sonli egri chiziqlar, massalar, tezliklar va energetik ko‘rsatkichlar — ochiq shartli model hisoblari; real o‘lchov yoki reyting emas.
- SiO₂ yemirish tezlik qonuni ayniqlashtirilgan sharoitga (shisha turi, harorat, aralashuv) tegishli; adabiyotdagi boshqa qiymatlar boshqa sharoitga tegishli.
- pK_a va gidratatsiya qiymatlari nashr ma’lumotlari; konsentratsiyalangan HF uchun spetsiatsiya modeli faolik koeffitsiyentlarni hisobga olmaydi.
- Elektrod potensial va doza tavsiyalari keltirilmagan: redoks xossalari faqat stöchiometriya orqali, xavfsizlik — stoksiometrik model orqali berilgan.
- Qurilma, texnologik parametrlar, konsentratsiya va idish materiali tanlash hamda amaliy tayyorlash bo‘yicha amaliyot tavsiyalari berilmaydi; xavfli kislotalar bilan ishlash tartibi 12-bo‘limda.
