# 6.5. HI — yodid kislota — manba va rasm izohi

## Manbalar (to‘liq o‘qilgan)
1. **Wikipedia — Hydroiodic acid** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroiodic_acid). Sanoat navi 48–57 % mass ulushi; Cativa jarayonida metanol va CO dan sirya olishda HI kofizayqin; 4HI + O₂ → 2H₂O + 2I₂ (havodan oksidlanish, namunalar qorayishi); nitroarenlarni anilinlarga qaytarish (qizil fosfor, 2001-yilda takomillashtirilgan usul); HI AQSh (DEA) I ro‘yxatida; suvda kislotalik HF dan kuchli; gidridi qaytarilishi mumkin.
2. **Wikipedia — Hydrogen iodide** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_iodide). M = 127,912 g/mol; Tb −35,36 °C; Tm −50,80 °C; suyuq zichligi 2,85 g/mL; kritik nuqta 151 °C / 8,31 MPa; eruvchanlik ≈245 g/100 mL (10 °C); ΔfH°(g) +26,5 kJ/mol; ΔfG°(g) +1,7 kJ/mol; S°(g) 206,6 J/(mol·K); Cp 29,2 J/(mol·K); sintez yo‘llari: 2I₂ + N₂H₄ → 4HI + N₂, H₂S + I₂ → 2HI + S, H₂ + I₂ ⇌ 2HI (taxminan 578 nm), 3I₂ + 2P + 6H₂O → 6HI + 2H₃PO₃, I₂ + tetralin usuli.
3. **Wikipedia — Iodine compounds** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iodine_compounds). HI azeotropi 56,7 % / 126,7 °C; polyiodidlar: I₂ + I⁻ ⇌ I₃⁻, K ≈ 700 (20 °C); yodidlar kuchli ligand sifatida [HgI₄]²⁻, [PbI₄]²⁻, [BiI₄]⁻, [AgI₂]⁻ komplekslarini beradi.
4. **Wikipedia — Iodine** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iodine). Jahon yod ishlab chiqarishi ≈30 000 t/y; Chili kalichida ≈23 000 t ( azot yondash mahsuloti), Yaponiya ≈9 000 t (brinlardan); manbalar taqsimoti: Chili kalichi ≈54 %, brinlar ≈43 %.
5. **ICSC 1326 — Hydrogen iodide** (https://www.inchem.org/pages/publications/?id=1326; ILO/WHO International Chemical Safety Card). CAS 10034-85-2; EC 233-109-9; UN 2197 (2.3 + 8); gidriod kislota UN 1787, klass 8; GHS «DANGER» — teri va ko‘zda kuchli kuyish, nafas yo‘llarining tirikligi, idish bosim ostida bo‘lishi xavfi; TLV 0,01 ppm (TWA); MAK — teri orqali absorbsiya belgisi; suyuq bilan teri uchmasligi (frostbite) xavfi.
6. **NIOSH IDLH Value Profile — Hydrogen iodide (Publication 2025-109, 2025-yil)**. IDLH 35 ppm / 183 mg/m³ (2010-yildagi 30 ppm qiymatidan ko‘tarilgan); 1 ppm HI = 5,23 mg/m³; AEGL-1 1,0 ppm, AEGL-2 150 ppm, AEGL-3 740 ppm (10 daqiqa).
7. **OSHA va NIOSH — yod (Iodine) chegaralari.** OSHA va NIOSH 0,1 ppm (ceiling); ACGIH TLV 0,01 ppm (TWA) va 0,1 ppm (STEL); IDLH (I₂) 2 ppm; NIOSH Pocket Guide (npgd0666).
8. **Cheméo/NIST va halogen jadvali (uWimona; Grokipedia)**. r(H–I) = 1,609 Å; D(H–I) ≈ 299 kJ/mol (NIST ma’lumoti; kitobda guruh jadvali bilan 295 kJ/mol); μ = 0,448–0,45 D; ionlanish potensiali 10,39 eV; yod o‘lchangan muz (222 K), qaynash (237,5 K) va atom radiusi (161 pm).
9. **USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries — Iodine; ATSDR Toxicological Profile for Iodine.** Jahon ishlab chiqarishi ≈30–33 kt/y; yod sikli: dengiz suvi, cho‘l tuzlari va tirik organizmlar; I-131 (yarim umr ≈8 kuni) va I-129 (uzun yarim umr) maxsus nazorat ostida.
10. **6.1-bo‘limdagi guruh ma’lumotlari (kitob ichki manbai).** D(H–X) = 569 / 432 / 366 / 295 kJ/mol; pK_a(suv) = 3,17 / −5,9 / −8,8 / −9,5; pK_a(DMSO) = 15 / −2,0 / −6,8 / −10,9; dipol momentlar 1,83 / 1,11 / 0,83 / 0,45 D; qaynash haroratlari 19,5 / −85,1 / −66,8 / −35,4 °C.

## Hisoblar va shartli taxminlar (barchasi ochiq shartli, o‘lchov ma’lumoti emas)
- **Taxmin A — suvli HI spetsiatsiyasi** (ideal erituvchi sharoiti; K_a = 3,2×10⁹ (pK_a −9,5), HI + I⁻ ⇌ HI₂⁻ lgK = 0,7; zaryad h = [I⁻] + [HI₂⁻] va massa c = [HI] + [I⁻] + 2[HI₂⁻] bo‘yicha sonikli yechim):
  0,100 M → I⁻ 100,000 %, pH 1,000; 0,500 M → pH 0,301; 1,000 M → pH 0,000; 2,000 M → pH −0,301; 4,000 M → pH −0,602; 6,000 M → pH −0,778; 7,560 M (azeotrop) → pH −0,879; 8,000 M → pH −0,903; 10,000 M → pH −1,000; 12,000 M → pH −1,079, HI₂⁻ ulushi 0,00002 %.
  Shartli hisob chegarasi: faolik koeffitsiyentlar, suv faolligi va gidronium agregatlari hisobga olinmaydi; konsentratsiyalangan eritmada apparent pH termodinamik pH dan farq qiladi.
- **Taxmin B — to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri usul** (H₂ + I₂ ⇌ 2HI; ΔfH°(HI g) = +26,5 kJ/mol, ΔfG°(HI g) = +1,7 kJ/mol → ΔrH = −9,44 kJ / 2 mol HI, K(298 K) = 0,254): 1000 kg HI → 7,88 kg H₂ va 992,1 kg I₂; to‘liq konversiya kutilmaydi.
- **Taxmin C — gidrazin usuli** (2I₂ + N₂H₄ → 4HI + N₂): 1000 kg HI uchun 992,1 kg I₂ va 62,6 kg N₂H₄.
- **Taxmin D — fosfor usuli** (3I₂ + 2P + 6H₂O → 6HI + 2H₃PO₃): 1000 kg HI uchun 992,1 kg I₂, 80,7 kg P, 140,8 kg H₂O va 213,7 kg H₃PO₃.
- **Taxmin E — H₂S usuli** (H₂S + I₂ → 2HI + S): 992,1 kg I₂, 133,2 kg H₂S va 125,3 kg S.
- **Taxmin F — yod olish sikli** (2NaI + Cl₂ → 2NaCl + I₂): 1000 kg NaI + 236,5 kg Cl₂ → 846,6 kg I₂ + 389,9 kg NaCl (kirish 1236,5 kg = chiqish 1236,5 kg).
- **Taxmin G — oksidlanish yo‘llari**: 4HI + O₂ → 2I₂ + 2H₂O (1000 kg HI uchun 992,1 kg I₂ va 62,5 kg O₂); 6HI + 2HNO₃ → 3I₂ + 2NO + 4H₂O (992,1 kg I₂ va 164,2 kg HNO₃).
- **Taxmin H — triiodid ion** (I₂ + I⁻ ⇌ I₃⁻, K ≈ 700; 1 L eritmada 1 g I₂ eritilganda): I₃⁻ ulushi — 0,10 M: 98,54 %; 0,50 M: 99,71 %; 1,00 M: 99,86 %; 2,00 M: 99,93 %; 4,00 M: 99,96 %; 6,00 M: 99,98 %; 7,56 M: 99,98 %; 10,0 M: 99,99 %; 12,0 M: 99,99 %.
- **Taxmin I — metall yemirilishi** (1 g metall): Fe → FeI₂ 5,545 g, HI 4,581 g, H₂ 0,036 g; Zn → ZnI₂ 4,882 g, HI 3,913 g, H₂ 0,031 g; Al → AlI₃ 15,110 g, HI 14,222 g, H₂ 0,112 g; Mg → MgI₂ 11,443 g, HI 10,526 g, H₂ 0,083 g; Cu → CuI₂ 4,994 g, HI 4,026 g, O₂ 0,252 g (2Cu + O₂ + 4HI → 2CuI₂ + 2H₂O).
- **Taxmin J — neytralizatsiya** (1000 kg 10 % li HI): Na₂CO₃ bilan Na₂CO₃ 41,4 kg, NaI 117,2 kg, CO₂ 17,2 kg, H₂O 7,0 kg (kirish 141,4 kg = chiqish 141,4 kg); Ca(OH)₂ bilan Ca(OH)₂ 29,0 kg, CaI₂ 114,9 kg, H₂O 14,1 kg (kirish 129,0 kg = chiqish 129,0 kg).
- **Taxmin K — yodometriya** (IO₃⁻ + 5I⁻ + 6H⁺ → 3I₂ + 3H₂O): 1,000 g KIO₃ → 0,712 g I₂ va 0,887 g tiosulfat.
- **Taxmin L — titrimetrik standartlash**: 0,2000 g Na₂CO₃ + 30,00 mL HI → C(HI) = 0,12580 mol/L.
- **Taxmin M — yodlangan tuz**: 1 t tuzga 30 g I qo‘shiladi — 39,2 g KI yoki 50,6 g KIO₃.
- **Fizik konstantalar**: M 127,9125 g/mol; Tb −35,36 °C; Tm −50,80 °C; T_k 151 °C; p_k 8,31 MPa; ρ_suyuq 2,85 g/cm³; ρ_gaz(25 °C) 5,23 g/L; d_gaz/havo 4,4; bug‘ bosimi 7,81 atm; eruvchanlik 42 g/100 mL (20 °C) va 234 g/100 mL (10 °C); azeotrop 56,7 % / 126,7 °C / ρ 1,70 / 7,56 mol/L; konsentratsiyalangan nav 48–57 %.

## Cheklovlar
- Sonli egri chiziqlar, massalar va energetik ko‘rsatkichlar — ochiq shartli hisoblar; real o‘lchov yoki reyting emas.
- Spetsiatsiya shartli hisobi faolik koeffitsiyentlarni hisobga olmaydi; konsentratsiyalangan eritmalarda apparent pH termodinamik pH dan farq qiladi.
- Yod ishlab chiqarish ko‘rsatkichlari (davlatlar kesimidagi ulushlar, 2024–2025 yillari) — bozor statistikasi; aniq zavod ma’lumotlari emas.
- Xavfsizlik chegaralari (ICSC 1326, NIOSH IDLH 2025-109, AEGL) — rasmiy tavsiya va chegaralar; individual kasb kasalligi bahosi emas.
- Ekologik ko‘rsatkichlar (yod sikli, endemik bo‘q, radioyod) — global baholashlar; mahalliy sharoitga bog‘liq emas.
- Qurilma, texnologik parametrlar, konsentratsiya va idish materiali tanlash hamda tibbiy dozalar bo‘yicha amaliyot tavsiyalari berilmaydi; xavfli kislotalar bilan ishlash tartibi 12-bo‘limda.
