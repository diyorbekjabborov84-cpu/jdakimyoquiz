# 5.3. Quritish va suvsizlantirish — manba va rasm izohi

## Manbalar (to‘liq o‘qilgan)
1. **OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, Appendix G “Standard Thermodynamic Properties”** — https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/g-standard-thermodynamic-properties-for-selected-substances . ΔfH° (25 °C): H₂O(l) −285,83; H₂O(g) −241,82 kJ/mol. Shundan ΔvapH(25 °C) = 44,01 kJ/mol.
2. **Ph. Eur. 6.0, 2.2.32 — Loss on drying** — http://www.uspbpep.com/ep60/2.2.32.%20loss%20on%20drying%2020232e.pdf . «Loss on drying is the loss of mass expressed as per cent m/m»; namuna oldindan quritilgan idishga solinadi va quyidagi usullardan biri bilan doimiy massaga qadar quritiladi: (a) P₂O₅ ustida, atmosferada, xona haroratida; (b) P₂O₅ ustida, 1,5–2,5 kPa vakuumda, xona haroratida; (c) P₂O₅ ustida, 1,5–2,5 kPa da, monografiyadagi harorat oralig‘ida; (d) shkafda, monografiyadagi harorat oralig‘ida; (e) 0,1 kPa dan katta bo‘lmagan chuqur vakuumda. Harorat bitta qiymat bilan ko‘rsatilgan bo‘lsa ±2 °C.
3. **Ph. Eur. 6.0, 5.11 — Characters section in monographs** — http://www.uspbpep.com/ep60/5.11.%20characters%20section%20in%20monographs%2051100e.pdf . Higroskopiklik sinovi: 50 mm diametr, 15 mm balandlikdagi shkaf; m₁ (idish va tiqin), m₂ (modda bilan), m₃ (24 soatdan keyin); sharoit — 25 ± 1 °C va 80 ± 2 % nisbiy namlik (yoki to‘yingan NH₄Cl/(NH₄)₂SO₄ eritmasi bilan desikator); Δm = (m₃ − m₂)/(m₂ − m₁) × 100 %; sinflar: deliquescent (suyuq hosil bo‘ladi), juda higroskopik (≥ 15 %), higroskopik (2–15 %), biroz higroskopik (0,2–2 %).
4. **Ph. Eur. 6.0, 2.5.12 (semi-micro Karl Fischer) va 2.5.32 (micro usul)** — suvning 1:1 stixiometrik titratsiyasi; moslik sinovi (namuna tarkibidagi suv 2,5 mg dan kam bo‘lsa, suv qo‘shib tekshiriladi).
5. **USP <731> — Loss on Drying** — «doimiy massaga qadar» iborasi: ketma-ket ikki tarozidagi farq 1 g modda hisobiga 0,50 mg dan oshmasligi kerak.
6. **U.S. EPA Method 9000 — Determination of Water by Karl Fischer Titration** — https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-12/documents/9000.pdf . 1 mol H₂O ga 1 mol I₂ to‘g‘ri keladi.
7. **IUPAC Gold Book** — «deliquescence» (D01582): atmosferaning namligi shunday bo‘lganda modda suv bug‘ini yig‘ib oxir-oqibat eritma hosil qilishi; «efflorescence» (E01909): kristall gidratning suv bug‘ bosimi atrof-muhit suv bug‘idan katta bo‘lganda kristallizatsiya suvini yo‘qotishi; «desiccant» (D01618): atrofida quruq holatni saqlovchi higroskopik modda.
8. **Azeotrop va molekulyar sital ma’lumotlari** — azeotroplar: toluen–suv 84,1 °C, suv 19,8 % (x = 0,444); sikloheksan–suv 69,5 °C, suv 8,4 % (x = 0,300); n-geptan–suv 70,9 °C, suv 12,5 % (x = 0,376); heteroazeotropning yuqori fazasi ~99,95 % entrainer, pastki fazasi ~99,95 % suv. Molekulyar sitalar: 3A (3 Å, suv 19–20 %), 4A (4 Å, 20–21 %), 5A (5 Å, 20–21 %), 13X (10 Å, 23–24 %); suvning kinetik diametri ~2,6 Å.
9. **CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics, 97th ed.** — ΔvapH(H₂O) = 40,657 kJ/mol (100 °C), ΔvapH(toluen) = 38,06 kJ/mol, ΔsubH(H₂O, muz) = 51,06 kJ/mol; molar massalar: H₂O 18,015; CuSO₄ 159,61; H₂SO₄ 98,08; HNO₃ 63,01; P₂O₅ 141,94; CaCl₂ 110,98; MgSO₄ 120,37.

## Hisoblar va shartli taxminlar (barchasi ochiq shartli)
- **Namlik ko‘rsatkichlari.** X = m(suv)/m(namuna), w = m(suv)/m(quruq modda), X = w/(1 + w). Taxminiy hisob: 0,35 kg suv + 1,00 kg quruq modda → X = 0,2593, w = 0,350. Quruq qoldig‘ (2.2.32): 10,00 g → 9,95 g → 0,50 % m/m.
- **Higroskopiklik (5.11).** Δm = (m₃ − m₂)/(m₂ − m₁) × 100 %; taxminiy hisob: m₁ = 5,00 g, m₂ = 15,00 g, m₃ = 15,35 g → 3,50 % → «higroskopik» sinf.
- **Karl Fischer.** n(I₂) = c·V/1000 = 0,50 × 22,2/1000 = 0,0111 mol; m(H₂O) = 0,0111 × 18,015 = 0,19997 g; 2,000 g namunda → 10,00 % suv.
- **Bug‘lanish termodinamikasi.** ΔvapH = ΔfH°(g) − ΔfH°(l) = 44,01 kJ/mol (25 °C); p_sat(25 °C) = 2,858 kPa; 100 °C da 40,657 kJ/mol. Klausius–Klapeiron (ΔvapH doimiy): 101,325 kPa → 100,0 °C; 10,0 kPa → 43,96 °C; 2,5 kPa → 17,80 °C; 1,5 kPa → 9,22 °C; 0,1 kPa → −28,96 °C.
- **Muvozanat namligi.** w_eq = w_m·C·x/(1 + (C − 1)x), x = p/p₀, w_m = 0,05 kg/kg, C = 10: 20 % RH → 0,0357; 50 % → 0,0455; 80 % → 0,0488; 95 % → 0,0497 kg/kg.
- **QuRITish kinetikasi bo‘yicha shartli hisob.** Ikki bosqichli: doimiy tezlik (X > X_c) va birinchi pasayish bosqichi (X_e ga yaqinlashuv). X₀ = 0,35, X_c = 0,20, X_e = 0,02, k₁ = 0,015 min⁻¹, k₂ = 0,080 min⁻¹: X(0) = 0,35; X(10) = 0,20; X(20) = 0,1009; X(40) = 0,0363. Haroratga bog‘lash: k(T) = k(373 K)·exp[−Eₐ/R(1/T − 1/373)], Eₐ = 30 kJ/mol.
- **QuRITgich sig‘imlari.** P₂O₅ + 3H₂O → 3H₃PO₄: 141,94 g → 54,05 g suv = 38,08 % massaga. CaCl₂·6H₂O: 0,974 kg suv/kg; MgSO₄·7H₂O: 1,048 kg suv/kg. Molekulyar sital: 3A 19,5 %; 4A 20,5 %; 5A 21,0 %; 13X 23,5 %. 100,0 kg suvni 4A bilan yig‘ish: 487,8 kg sital.
- **Gidrat quritish.** CuSO₄·5H₂O (249,68) → CuSO₄·3H₂O (213,65) → CuSO₄·H₂O (177,62) → CuSO₄ (159,61). Taxminiy hisob: 1000,0 kg pentahidrat → n = 4,0051 kmol, suv 288,60 kg, monohidrat 711,40 kg, suv ulushi 36,08 % → 10,14 %; anhiddirgacha: suv 360,75 kg, mahsulot 639,25 kg.
- **Azeotropik quritish.** Toluen–suv 84,1 °C, bug fazasida suv 19,8 % (1 kg toluen → 0,2469 kg suv); sikloheksan 0,0917 kg/kg; geptan 0,1429 kg/kg. Taxminiy hisob (1000 kg 68 % li HNO₃ → suv 0,5 %): kislota 680,0 kg; mahsulot 683,42 kg; chiqarilgan suv 316,58 kg; distillat 1598,90 kg; toluen 1282,32 kg; suv fazasi 316,74 kg, organik faza 1282,16 kg; toluen yo‘qotish 0,158 kg.
- **Energetika.** 1 kg suvni bug‘latish: 2,2568 MJ = 0,6269 kWh. Taxmin A: 288,60 kg suv → 651,3 MJ (180,9 kWh). Taxmin B: 316,58 kg suv → 714,5 MJ va 1282,32 kg toluen → 529,7 MJ, jami 1244,2 MJ (345,6 kWh). Usullar taqqoslash taxminiy hisobi (kWh/kg suv): molekulyar sital 0,18; vakuum 0,85; shkaf 1,05; azeotrop 1,55.

## Cheklovlar
- Sonli egri chiziqlar taxminiy hisoblar; real o‘lchov, reyting yoki texnologik parametr emas.
- Azeotrop qiymatlari va molekulyar sital sig‘imlari — nashr ma’lumotlari; namlik sharoiti, ifloslanish va regeneratsiya holatiga qarab o‘zgaradi.
- Ph. Eur. monografiyasi har bir kislota uchun alohida; bu bobda faqat umumiy usullar (2.2.32, 2.5.12, 5.11) keltirilgan.
- Quritishning texnologik parametrlari, quritish rejasi, konsentratsiya tanlash, idish materiali va saqlash bo‘yicha amaliyot tavsiyalari berilmagan; xavfsizlik faqat umumiy tamoyil darajasida.
