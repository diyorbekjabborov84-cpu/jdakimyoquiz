# 4.4. Kompleks va cho‘kma hosil qilish — manba va rasm izohi

## Manbalar (toliq o‘qilgan)
1. **OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, 15.1 “Precipitation and Dissolution”** — https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/15-1-precipitation-and-dissolution . Dissotsiatsiya tenglamasi va K_sp ifodalari; stoxiometriyaga qarab eruvchanlik formulalari (1:1 → s²=K_sp; 1:2 → 4s³=K_sp); Q_sp va K_sp taqqoslashining natijalari; cho‘kish qarorining qattiq faza mavjudligiga bog‘liqligi.
2. **OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, 15.2 “Lewis Acids and Bases”** — https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/15-2-lewis-acids-and-bases . Ligand elektrondagi juftni metall markazga beradi; hosil bo‘lish konstantasi K_f va dissotsiatsiya konstantasi K_d=1/K_f; Ag(NH₃)₂⁺ misoli (K_f=1,7×10⁷); komplekslanish tufayli AgCl ning erish mexanizmi (erkin Ag⁺ kamayishi → Q_sp<K_sp).
3. **IUPAC Gold Book, “Solubility product” (S05742)** — DOI 10.1351/goldbook.S05742 — https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/S05742 . Ta’rif: to‘yingan eritmada ion faolliklarining tegishli darajalar bilan ko‘paytmasidan iborat muvozanat konstantasi, dissotsiatsiya muvozanati va aynan shu tizimning ionlari hisobga olingan holda.
4. **IUPAC Gold Book, “Complex” (C01203)** — DOI 10.1351/goldbook.C01203 — https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/C01203 . Noorganik kimyoda «coordination entity» atamasining tavsiya etilishi; murakkab tushunchani aniqroq atama bilan almashtirish.
5. **IUPAC Gold Book, “Equilibrium constant” (E02177)** — DOI 10.1351/goldbook.E02177 — https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/E02177 . Muvozanat konstantasi tushunchasi, standart holat va faollikka bog‘liq ifodalar.
6. **OpenStax, Chemistry 2e, Appendix J (Solubility Products), Appendix K (Formation Constants for Complex Ions), Appendix H (Ionization Constants of Weak Acids)** — https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/j-solubility-products ; https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/k-formation-constants-for-complex-ions ; https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/h-ionization-constants-of-weak-acids . J1: AgCl 1,6×10⁻¹⁰; BaSO₄ 2,3×10⁻⁸; CaF₂ 4,0×10⁻¹¹; CaSO₄·2H₂O 6,1×10⁻⁵; Ca₃(PO₄)₂ 1,3×10⁻³²; Ag₂S 1,6×10⁻⁴⁹; PbS 7×10⁻²⁹; CuS 8,5×10⁻⁴⁵; HgS 1,6×10⁻⁵⁴; Al(OH)₃ 2×10⁻³²; Fe(OH)₃ 4×10⁻³⁸. K1: Ag(NH₃)₂⁺ 1,7×10⁷; Ag(CN)₂⁻ 1×10²¹; AgCl₂⁻ 1,8×10⁵; [AlF₆]³⁻ 7×10¹⁹; [HgCl₄]²⁻ 1,1×10¹⁶; [Fe(CN)₆]³⁻ 2×10⁴³; [Zn(OH)₄]²⁻ 2×10¹⁵; Cu(NH₃)₄²⁺ 1,7×10¹³; Fe(SCN)²⁺ 8,9×10². H1: HSO₄⁻ 1,2×10⁻²; HS⁻ 1,0×10⁻¹⁹; H₃PO₄ 7,5×10⁻³, 6,2×10⁻⁸, 4,2×10⁻¹³; H₂C₂O₄ 6,0×10⁻², 6,1×10⁻⁵.
7. **IUPAC Gold Book, “Precipitation from homogeneous solution” (P04797)** — DOI 10.1351/goldbook.P04797 — https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/P04797 . Cho‘kmaning sekin, homogen hosil bo‘lishi va shu bilan bog‘liq farqlar (faqat ta’rif darajasida).

## Hisoblar va shartli taxminlar (barchasi ochiq shartli)
- **Konstantalar.** Faqat jadvaldan olingan K_sp, β va K_a qiymatlari taxminiy hisoblarga kirgan; boshqa barcha qiymatlar taxminiy hisob ichida.
- **Erkin ligand bo‘yicha shartli hisob.** β₂,c=1,7×10⁷ L² mol⁻² (Ag(NH₃)₂⁺); K_sp,c=1,6×10⁻¹⁰ mol² L⁻² (AgCl); pK_a,c=9,25 (NH₄⁺). Ideal konsentratsion yaqinlashuv (γ_i≈1, c°=1 mol L⁻¹, 25 °C).
- **Amfoterlik bo‘yicha shartli hisob.** Xayoliy M³⁺/M(OH)₃/[M(OH)₄]⁻ tizimi: K_sp,c=10⁻³⁰, β₄,c=10³⁴. Minimum sharti β₄,c·x⁴=3 → pH≈5,619, C_sat≈5,55×10⁻⁵ mol L⁻¹. Bu haqiqiy alyuminiy uchun universal diagramma emas.
- **Tanlab cho‘ktirish bo‘yicha shartli hisob.** A²⁺: K_sp,c=10⁻¹⁸; B²⁺: 10⁻¹²; C_M,0=0,0100 mol L⁻¹; F_M=1 yoki 10⁴. Boshlanish pH 6,00/9,00; 99,9% pH 7,50; F_A=10⁴ holatida 8,00 va 9,50.
- **Ishlangan hisob.** AgCl qattiq fazasi ortiqcha, pH=10,00, C_B,0=0,0100 mol L⁻¹: l=0,00779980; s=0,000406984; [Ag⁺]=3,93136×10⁻⁷; [Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺=0,000406591 mol L⁻¹; kompleks ulushi 99,903%; ligand sarfini e’tiborsiz qoldirishda s=0,000442976 (+8,84%).
- **Atom va zaryad balansi.** 7 ta reagent tenglamasi mustaqil element/zaroyad tekshiruvidan o‘tgan: AgCl⇌Ag⁺+Cl⁻; Ag⁺+2NH₃⇌Ag(NH₃)₂⁺; HSO₄⁻⇌H⁺+SO₄²⁻; S²⁻+2H⁺⇌H₂S; CaSO₄⇌Ca²⁺+SO₄²⁻; Al(OH)₃+3H⁺⇌Al³⁺+3H₂O; Al(OH)₃+OH⁻⇌Al(OH)₄⁻.

## Cheklovlar
- Sonli egri chiziqlar taxminiy hisoblar; real metall–kislota tajriba ma’lumoti yoki materiallar reytingi emas.
- Sxemalar mexanizmni tasvirlamaydi; faqat termodinamik yo‘nalishni ko‘rsatadi.
- Moddalar konsentratsiyasi va pH oralig‘i real eksperiment, idish materiali tanlash yoki xavfsizlik qoidalari uchun ko‘rsatma emas. Tsianid va sulfid bilan bog‘liq xavfli jarayonlar muhokama qilinadi, texnologiya berilmaydi.
- Faollik koeffitsientlari γ_i≈1 deb olingan; kuchli ion juftlanishida konsentratsion hisob yetarli emas.
